Pesto Sauce

Description

One of the best pasta/pizza sauces in my opinion, pesto is relatively easy to make and takes very little time. It's also very addictive and easy to snack too much on while making the rest of your dish. Though the ingredients can get a little pricey, so I'd recommend having your own basil bush for some fresh basil!

As always, measure with your heart and taste as you go!

Ingredients

  • 1-2 oz of basil
  • 4 oz bag of pine nuts
  • 2-3 oz of grated parmesan
  • olive oil
  • lemon juice
  • salt

Steps

  1. Start by getting your blender or food processor out and set up.
  2. Add in all of your basil. If you got store bought then be sure to pick through and discard all of the discolored pieces.
  3. Next add in the entire bag of pine nuts, almost all of your parmesan, a few pinches of salt and a good squirt of lemon juice. Then pour a good amount of olive oil on top.
  4. Blend on high until everything is incorporated. You may have to add more oil if the mixture seems too dry and is clumping together a bit. Add more than you think you need to, you want everything to be well lubricated.
  5. Taste your sauce. Add more salt, lemon juice or parmesan as needed.
  6. Enjoy!
