Baked Mac N Cheese

Description

This delicious recipe is a staple for me and my family. It's super easy to prepeare, cooks in a relatively short period of time, and provides leftovers for days! It's also very customizable as you can add veggetables like broccoli and peas, or other good stuff like bacon and breadcrumbs.

For the best results I recommend you hand grate your cheese. And as far as spices go, measure with your heart <3

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 4 cups milk
  • 8 oz grated sharp cheddar
  • 8 oz grated medium cheddar
  • 8 oz grated gouda
  • 1 lb elbow macaroni noodles
  • salt
  • pepper
  • peprika
  • dijon mustard
  • any additional additives

Steps

  1. Start by preheating your oven to 375 degrees and spraying your baking dish with a non-stick spray.
  2. The first thing we are going to make is the noddles. Cook them until they are slightly underdone, about 1 or 2 minutes less than the recommended time on the package, then strain. I usually let them hang out in the strainer until I'm ready to add them to the roux.
  3. To make your roux, first melt your butter in a medium pot on low heat. You can use the same pot you cooked the noddles in, you may just have to run it under some cold water to cool it down. Then add your flour, stir and cook for about 1 minute until it is golden brown and resembles wet sand.
  4. Slowly add in your milk 1/2 cup at a time while continuously stirring. For the first 2 cups make sure you fully combine the milk with the roux before adding the next 1/2 cup.
  5. Once you've added all of the milk, raise the heat to medium and let the sauce reduce until it's thick enough to coat the back of a spoon or until it's the consistencey you desire. Be sure to stir often.
  6. Once the roux has reduced to your liking, add a little over half of your shredded cheese a handful at a time and stir until it melts. Be sure to leave enough for 2 layers over your mac n cheese, one in the middle and one on top.
  7. Next, add all of your spices and any other additives you wish. If you plan to add breadcrumbs I would wait until you're sprinkling the cheese on top.
  8. Now it's time to add the cooked noodles to the roux. Stir until everything is evenly combined.
  9. Pour half of your mac n cheese into the baking dish and sprinkle half of your remaining shredded cheese over top. Additionally here you can add some breadcrumbs. Then pour the remaining mac n cheese into the dish and spread your remaining cheese over top, also adding breadcrumbs if desired.
  10. Bake in the oven for about 30 minutes or until the cheese on top is melted and browning in spots.
  11. Enjoy!
