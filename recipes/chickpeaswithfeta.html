Chickpeas with Feta

Description

Chickpeas are a great source of protien and fiber, making them a good choice for a side or even the main dish. This recipe isn't too difficult and takes a relatively short time to make. If you plan on using a chicken broth, I recommend subbing out for better than bouillon!

Remember to measure with your heart, and most importantly, taste as you go!

Ingredients

  • olive oil
  • (optional) red pepper flakes
  • 1 (15 oz) can of chickpeas
  • 1 large shallot
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1/2 cup vegetable or chicken broth
  • feta cheese
  • lemon or lime juice
  • salt
  • pepper
  • (optional) fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Start by chopping up your fresh garlic and shallot and rinsing your chickpeas. You want to dice the shallot and chop the garlic into thin slices.
  2. Add some olive oil to a medium skillet and cook your garlic, and optionally a pinch of red pepper flakes, over low/medium heat for about a minute until the garlic becomes pale gold.
  3. Next, stir in your shallot and cook for about 3 minutes until it's softened and lightly browned.
  4. Then, add in your chickpeas and broth. Cover and cook for a couple minutes until the chickpeas have warmed through.
  5. Once warmed, uncover and simmer on medium/high heat for a couple minutes until the liquid has reduced slightly. Be careful not to turn the heat up too much or simmer for too long otherwise you'll lose your broth.
  6. Take everyhing off the heat and add your feta, lemon/lime juice, salt, pepper, and optionally some fresh parsley. Taste and add more seasonings or feta as desired.
  7. Enjoy!
